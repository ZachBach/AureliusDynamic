Privacy Policy

echoGalaxy is a free educational app for exploring the universe. This policy is short because there is genuinely nothing to disclose.

What we collect

Nothing. echoGalaxy has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no cookies. It does not request any device permissions. It collects, stores, and transmits no personal information of any kind.

How the app works

Everything in echoGalaxy — every planet, nebula, and galaxy — is generated on your own device. After the first visit the app can run entirely offline. Network requests are made only to load the app itself from our website.

Children

echoGalaxy is designed to be safe for everyone, including children. Since no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children.
